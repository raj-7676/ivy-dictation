# Ivy name and logo

Ivy's code is open. Its name and logo are not.

"Ivy", "Ivy Dictation" and the Ivy logo are trademarks of Glow.co. The license ([LICENSE](LICENSE)) lets you
use, change and share Ivy's code. It does not give you the name or the logo.

## You may

- Say in plain text that your project is "based on Ivy" or "a fork of Ivy".
- Share unchanged official Ivy builds under the Ivy name, with a link to the official download.
- Write about Ivy, review it and show screenshots of it.

## You may not

- Release a changed copy of Ivy as "Ivy" or "Ivy Dictation", or with the Ivy logo or anything that looks like it.
  Give your version its own name and its own icon.
- Publish Ivy, changed or not, in any app store. Only Glow.co publishes Ivy in app stores.
- Suggest that Glow.co made, checked or supports your version.
